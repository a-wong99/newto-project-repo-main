In this project, I assumed the role of a Data Analyst at a fictional company called Summit Outfitters.

My goal was to analyse the given data, and answer the following business questions:

• How much revenue, cost, and profit has Summit made — and what is the profit margin?

• Is the business growing? (revenue by year, and year-over-year growth)

• Which product categories and which regions drive the most profit?

• Are we on track against this year's revenue target of $1,550,000 (the FY2024 goal)?

• Where are our customers, geographically?

• Which customer segments and sales channels matter most?

• When in the year does Summit sell the most? (seasonality)

The presentation contains descriptions of the full workflow, from data cleaning to data exploration all the way to analysis and visualisations.
