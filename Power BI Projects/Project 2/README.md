In this project, I assumed the role of a data analyst at a fictional subscription company called Novastream.

The brief stated that the company is worried about customer churn, and has given me the task of looking at their customer data to analyse churn factors.

The project objectives were:
- Prepare the data: find and fix the quality issues across the tables so they are analysis-ready.
- Model the data: build the relationships into a star schema and add a Date table.
- Analyse & visualise: explore the data and build visuals that reveal what drives churn.
- Communicate: present clear insights and actionable recommendations to a business audience.

The key questions to answer were:
1. What is the overall churn rate, and how many customers does it represent?

2. Does contract type (monthly vs annual) relate to churn?

3. Do auto-renew, plan, or price relate to churn?

4. Do engagement signals — usage hours, days since last login, tenure, support tickets- separate customers who churn from those who stay?

5. Which factors matter most, and which look like they matter but don’t?
